CNES Nuclear Week 2026

  • November 2–6, 2026
  • Montreal

CNES Nuclear Week is a week-long series of lectures, virtual visits, webinars and activities introducing students to nuclear engineering, nuclear energy and the broader nuclear industry.

Throughout the week, students will have the opportunity to learn about topics including nuclear fundamentals, radiation and detection, CANDU technology and the Canadian nuclear industry, nuclear safety, careers and emerging technologies.

One of the confirmed highlights of the week is a CMS Virtual Visit at CERN on November 3 at 10:00 AM, giving students the opportunity to connect live with members of the CMS experiment and learn more about one of the world's largest particle physics experiments.

CNES is also coordinating lectures and virtual sessions with professionals and organizations from the Canadian nuclear sector. Additional speakers, sessions, locations and registration details will be announced as they are confirmed.

Planned program

November 2 — Nuclear 101 / Introduction to Nuclear Engineering
November 3 — CMS Virtual Visit at CERN — 10:00 AM
November 4 — CANDU Technology and the Canadian Nuclear Industry
November 5 — Nuclear Safety, Careers and Emerging Technologies
November 6 — Additional activities / closing events

More details and registration coming soon.
